# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Mobile players who want a tactile challenge that requires patience and spatial memory. They play alone, in quiet or dark environments, phone in hand. The session is deliberate: they settle in, start a run, and drag one finger across the black screen feeling for danger. They are not looking for a skinner-box reward loop. They want the specific feeling of navigating something they cannot see.

## Product Purpose

BlindSweeper is a minefield game played entirely by feel. The board is black. You drag to sense proximity through haptic vibration. You tap to mark what you think is a mine. Drag into an unmarked mine and you lose. Mark every mine and you advance to a harder level. The game exists to deliver a specific physical sensation: earned discovery through tension and restraint.

## Brand Personality

Tense. Precise. Unforgiving.

Voice: spare, confident, no fluff. The UI does not cheer you on. It reports facts. Labels are nouns and numbers, not affirmations.

Tone: calm before something goes wrong. The design should feel like the moment before an explosion, not after.

## Anti-references

- **Polished SaaS dashboard**: no card grids, no sidebar nav, no metric tiles with supporting labels, no gradient-accent stats panels.
- **Casual mobile game**: no bouncy enter animations, no bright reward colors, no star/coin iconography, no playful rounded everything.
- **RGB gaming**: no neon-on-black, no glowing borders, no purple-to-cyan gradients, no "gaming" typography.

## Design Principles

1. **Darkness is the mechanic.** The black board is not a style choice; it is the game. UI chrome should feel like an extension of that darkness, not a frame around it.
2. **Restraint creates tension.** Every withheld element is a deliberate choice. Absence communicates as much as presence.
3. **Information on demand.** Stats and feedback surface in response to action. The home screen shows nothing until there is something worth showing.
4. **The board is the product.** Status elements exist to orient, not to compete. During play, everything yields to the board.
5. **Precision over decoration.** Calibrated spacing, deliberate type scale, no softening curves. The UI should feel like it was assembled with the same care the player needs to win.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast for all text. Haptics are progressive enhancement: the full game is playable via audio fallback and visual proximity meter when vibration is unavailable. Reduced-motion preference should suppress non-essential transitions.
